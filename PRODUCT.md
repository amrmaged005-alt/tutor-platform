# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coursaty serves students and parents in Egypt who need to find and book suitable classes with confidence. It also serves tutors and learning centers managing classes, students, bookings, payouts, and messages. Users may browse in English or Arabic, on desktop or mobile, and often need to make a practical decision quickly.

## Product Purpose

Coursaty is a local tutoring marketplace that makes classes easier to discover, compare, and book. It brings verified tutors, curricula, schedules, locations, formats, prices, remaining seats, reviews, and booking flows into one clear system. Success means users can move from a learning need to a confident booking without chasing scattered messages or guessing at details.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, local. The product should feel clear and considered, with enough warmth to suit education and enough restraint to keep attention on the next useful action.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative glassmorphism, loud gradients, neon accents, excessive card nesting, and ornamental motion that slows down task completion. Avoid interfaces that feel imported from another market without regard for Arabic, RTL behavior, local curricula, and Egypt-specific booking needs.

## Design Principles

1. Make trust visible through verified profiles, reviews, clear details, and predictable actions.
2. Keep the next useful action obvious, especially while browsing, comparing, and booking.
3. Design for Egypt as the default context, including local curricula, EGP pricing, English and Arabic, and RTL layouts.
4. Use warmth with restraint so the interface feels approachable without becoming decorative.
5. Preserve familiar product patterns across web and mobile so users can focus on their task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls. Preserve keyboard focus visibility, reduced-motion alternatives, semantic labels, touch-friendly hit areas, loading and empty states, English and Arabic localization, and complete RTL support.
